Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CGTool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information while you use our website and online tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use CGTool.

2. Information We Collect

CGTool's current tools are designed to work without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not intentionally ask users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, home address, or identification documents in order to use the basic tools.

3. Images and Files

Some CGTool tools allow you to select images or other files from your device.

The current image-processing tools are designed to process selected images locally in your web browser using technologies such as JavaScript, HTML Canvas, OpenCV.js, and other browser-based processing methods.

CGTool does not intentionally upload or store the selected images on a CGTool server as part of the normal operation of these tools.

Important: Because processing happens in your browser, you should still use a secure and trusted device and browser when working with sensitive documents or photographs.

4. Passport Photo Maker

The CGTool Passport Photo Maker allows users to select an image, crop and adjust it, change brightness and contrast, rotate or move the image, and generate downloadable photos or A4 print layouts.

These operations are performed using browser-based processing. The selected photograph is not intentionally sent to a CGTool server for storage.

5. Smart Document Scanner

The CGTool Smart Document Scanner allows users to select a photograph of a document and perform operations such as document detection, perspective correction, enhancement, JPG export, and A4 PDF generation.

These operations are performed in the user's browser using client-side technologies.

6. Third-Party Libraries

Some CGTool tools use third-party JavaScript libraries to provide specific features.

For example, the Smart Document Scanner currently uses:

  • OpenCV.js for image and document processing.
  • jsPDF for PDF generation.

These libraries may be loaded from third-party content delivery networks or external websites. Those third-party services may have their own policies and technical practices.

CGTool does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Cookies

The basic CGTool tools do not require cookies to perform their main functions.

If cookies or similar technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their use.

8. Advertising and Analytics

CGTool may introduce advertising, analytics, or other third-party services in the future.

If such services are added, they may collect technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy may be updated when such services are introduced.

9. Hosting and Technical Information

CGTool may be hosted using third-party hosting infrastructure, such as GitHub Pages.

Hosting providers may automatically process certain technical information required to deliver and secure websites. This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, date and time of access, and other technical information.

Such information may be handled according to the policies of the hosting provider.

10. External Links

CGTool may contain links to external websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites before using them.

11. Children's Privacy

CGTool is a general-purpose website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the basic CGTool tools.

12. Data Security

We aim to provide secure and privacy-conscious tools. Browser-based processing is used where possible so that selected images can be processed locally on the user's device.

However, no website, browser, device, or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CGTool may update this Privacy Policy when new tools, features, advertising services, analytics services, or other technologies are introduced.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date may be changed.

14. Contact

If you have questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact CGTool through the Contact Us page once it is available.

15. Your Use of CGTool

By accessing or using CGTool, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
