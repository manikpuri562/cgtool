About CGTool

Welcome to CGTool, a collection of simple, fast, and useful online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

Our goal is to provide easy-to-use tools that anyone can access directly from a web browser without complicated software or installations.

What We Offer

CGTool is being developed to provide useful tools such as:

  • Image and PDF tools
  • Passport photo tools
  • Image conversion and resizing tools
  • File and document utilities
  • Other simple online productivity tools

We are continuously working to add more useful tools and improve the experience for our users.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make common digital tasks simple, fast, and accessible for everyone.

Free and Easy to Use

We aim to keep our tools simple and easy to use. Whenever possible, our tools are designed to work directly in your browser without requiring complicated software installation.
